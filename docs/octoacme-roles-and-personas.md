# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create user-centered designs, wireframes, and interaction flows. They collaborate closely with Product Managers and Developers to ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and meet user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Design wireframes and user interaction flows
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create design systems and component libraries
- Collaborate with Developers to ensure design feasibility
- Participate in design reviews and iterate based on feedback
- Document design decisions and specifications

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce friction and improve user satisfaction
- Ensure consistency across product interfaces
- Bridge the gap between user needs and technical implementation

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions with Product Managers and Developers
- Usability testing reports and insights
- Design specification documents and design system updates
- Collaboration on accessibility requirements and implementation
- Feedback incorporation in sprint planning

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Validate design solutions against user research and product goals
- **Developers**: Provide detailed specifications and collaborate on implementation feasibility
- **Project Managers**: Communicate design timelines and dependencies

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, and deployment automation. They work closely with Developers and Project Managers to enable reliable, rapid releases and operational stability.

### Responsibilities
- Design and implement CI/CD pipelines
- Manage deployment automation and orchestration
- Oversee infrastructure provisioning and scaling
- Monitor system health, performance, and uptime
- Implement logging, monitoring, and alerting systems
- Manage secrets, security configurations, and compliance
- Support incident response and post-incident analysis

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments with minimal manual effort
- Ensure system stability, scalability, and security
- Reduce deployment risk and recovery time
- Improve observability and operational efficiency

### Typical Communication
- Release coordination and deployment windows
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure capacity and performance reviews
- Documentation of runbooks and operational procedures
- Collaboration on CI/CD improvements

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Ensure pipelines support rapid iteration; provide feedback on deployment issues
- **Project Managers**: Communicate deployment readiness and capacity constraints
- **Security Lead**: Align on security controls, secrets management, and compliance requirements

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads identify, assess, and mitigate security risks throughout the project lifecycle. They collaborate with all teams to embed security practices, compliance, and incident response into project execution.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct threat modeling and security risk assessments
- Review code and architecture for security vulnerabilities
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Manage incident response and post-incident reviews
- Recommend security tools, practices, and training
- Participate in architecture and design reviews

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and minimize organizational risk
- Embed security into development processes early (shift-left)
- Ensure compliance with regulations and organizational policies
- Foster a security-aware culture across the team

### Typical Communication
- Security review sessions and threat assessments
- Vulnerability reports and remediation guidance
- Security incident escalation and response coordination
- Compliance documentation and audit support
- Security training and best practice guidance

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide security guidance, code review feedback, and training
- **DevOps Engineers**: Align on infrastructure security, secrets management, and access controls
- **Project Managers**: Escalate security risks and ensure remediation timelines
- **Product Managers**: Define security requirements for features and roadmap prioritization

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather, analyze, and synthesize business requirements and stakeholder feedback. They bridge business objectives and technical solutions, ensuring alignment and measurable outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Analyze processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Create requirements specifications and acceptance criteria
- Support trade-off analysis and decision-making
- Validate solutions meet business objectives
- Track success metrics and business outcomes

### Goals
- Ensure technical solutions align with business objectives
- Reduce miscommunication and rework between business and engineering
- Maximize return on investment and business value delivery
- Enable data-driven decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder workshops and requirement gathering sessions
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Trade-off analysis and recommendation presentations
- Metrics dashboards and outcome reporting
- Regular alignment with Product Managers and Project Managers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on roadmap prioritization and success metrics definition
- **Project Managers**: Support scope definition and change management
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements and validate technical feasibility
- **Stakeholders**: Gather input and communicate project progress and outcomes
